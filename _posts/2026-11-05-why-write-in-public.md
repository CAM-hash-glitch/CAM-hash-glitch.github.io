The case for writing in public is simple: writing is how you find out what you think, and publishing is how you find out whether you were right. A private notebook lets you believe you understand something; a public essay invites someone to show you where you don't.[^1]

## Two kinds of writing

This site has two sections:

- **Essays** are longer and slower. I try to make each one a complete argument: what I believe, why, how confident I am, and what would change my mind.
- **Notes** are shorter and faster. Links with a paragraph of commentary, observations, quick estimates, and ideas that aren't ready to be essays yet.

> The point of notes is to lower the cost of thinking out loud. The point of essays is to raise the standard of what gets said.

## What I'll try to do

1. Say what I actually think, with stated uncertainty.
2. Show the reasoning, not just the conclusion.
3. Update visibly when I'm wrong, rather than quietly editing.
