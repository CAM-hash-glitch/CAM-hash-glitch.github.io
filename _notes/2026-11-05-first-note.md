---
title: Notes are for thinking out loud
tags: [meta]
---

Notes are short posts. They appear in full on the [Notes](/notes/) page, newest first, in a running stream — so a note can be a single paragraph, a quote with a sentence of commentary, or a link and a question.

To write one, add a file to `_notes/` named like `2026-11-05-some-title.md`, give it a `title`, and push.
